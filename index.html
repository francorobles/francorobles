About Me

With nearly two decades of experience in systems architecture and full-stack development, I build robust, scalable solutions that connect product ideas, backend systems, cloud platforms, and real-world infrastructure. My work has spanned .NET, Node.js, Python, Java, Go, JavaScript, TypeScript, and Kotlin, along with databases such as SQL Server and PostgreSQL. I enjoy moving between hands-on implementation and high-level design, whether that means shaping APIs, leading platform decisions, or keeping complex production environments reliable.

Innovation, to me, is not about chasing every new framework. It is about applying the right tools with discipline to solve real problems. That mindset carries into my work with cloud systems, automation, and AI orchestration, where I explore practical ways to streamline delivery and reduce operational drag. Across projects, I have worked with technologies such as React, React Native, TypeScript, JavaScript, Python, Kotlin, Java, Go, PostgreSQL, and AWS, and I value being able to adapt the stack to the problem instead of forcing the problem to fit the stack.

My professional focus is complemented by a fascination with the precision and strategy found in high-stakes engineering disciplines, such as those seen in elite motorsport. I find that the principles of resilience and technical excellence required on the track mirror the same level of dedication I bring to complex software systems and production environments. Whether I am designing distributed services, improving frontend experiences, tuning backend performance, or configuring a remote network node, my goal remains the same: to deliver reliable engineering that holds up under pressure and creates real value for the people using it.

In addition to my technical pursuits, I am committed to the growth of the local tech landscape, frequently engaging in initiatives that bring hands-on technology experience to the next generation. I thrive in environments that demand both strategic oversight and granular technical execution, balancing the big-picture vision of a systems architect with the precision of a senior engineer. As I continue to evolve alongside the ever-shifting digital frontier, I remain focused on crafting software and infrastructure that are not only functional but truly transformative in their impact.
